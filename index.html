FAQ

Common questions

Can't find what you need? Call us or send a message on Facebook.

How far in advance should I book event standby?

As early as you can, especially for weekends and big events. Get in touch with your date and venue and we'll confirm availability.

How many ambulances does my event need?

It depends on crowd size, venue layout and the type of activity. Tell us about your event and we'll recommend the right setup.

Do you provide standby for company, school or work-site requirements?

Yes. We provide ambulance standby for companies, schools and work sites that need medical coverage.

Can you convert our existing van into an ambulance?

Yes. We've been converting vans into DOH-approved ambulances since 2010. Contact us with your vehicle model to discuss the build.
